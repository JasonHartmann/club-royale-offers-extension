**v2.5.0**
- All Offers is way snappier on big accounts! Groups build when you open them and scrolling reuses rows instead of redrawing the whole table, and the spinner stays up until a group is done filling in
- The active-filter panel folds away — tap the "N active" badge to toggle it, and it tucks itself in when you scroll
- Link as many accounts as you want. A row still pairs only two of the same brand
- Combined Offers: Union All now lists each account's own sailing, and the Profile column tells you who's on the row
- Cards keep each pair on its own card
- Back-to-Back: a paired row spends both players' offers, and each leg shows whose profile it belongs to
- Back-to-Back: Cheapest sorts by the upgrade price on the card, so that $0 balcony on the same ship jumps to the top
- Back-to-Back: "Same Room" now means same ship and same room — the green ones — and they lead the list
- Back-to-Back: the builder shows your true chain length past 15 (overachievers welcome!)
Bug fixes:
- Starring a combined sailing only stars that pair, not every linked account
- CSV: paired favorites export the right profiles, and Combined Offers keeps a single Profile column
- Offers that say "Apply $800 Off" an upgraded room (the amount varies) now show that dollar amount as the value, not the room's regular price. A better room only gets that amount off
- Safari (Mac): saving and loading no longer hang on a stuck native call
